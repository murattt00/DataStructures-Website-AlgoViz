"Understanding the Vision Behind AlgoViz"

📚 About AlgoViz

AlgoViz is a comprehensive web-based learning platform developed as a final project for the 3507 Computer Engineering course. The primary goal of this project is to bridge the gap between abstract theoretical concepts and practical understanding in the field of Data Structures and Algorithms.

🎯 Our Mission

Computer science concepts, particularly complex data structures like AVL Trees and recursive sorting algorithms, can be difficult to grasp through static textbook diagrams. AlgoViz aims to solve this by providing interactive, step-by-step visualizations that reveal the internal mechanics of these operations in real-time.
