How Bubble Sort Works

🔄 Compare & Swap

Repeatedly compares adjacent elements and swaps them if they are in wrong order.

📊 Multiple Passes

Each pass moves the largest unsorted element to its correct position at the end.

⏱️ Time Complexity

Best: O(n) when already sorted. Average & Worst: O(n²) - suitable for small datasets.

✅ Stable Sort

Maintains the relative order of equal elements. Simple to understand and implement.
