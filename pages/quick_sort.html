"Efficient Divide-and-Conquer Sorting Algorithm"

How Quick Sort Works

🎯 Pivot Selection

Selects a pivot element and partitions the array around it: smaller elements left, larger right.

🔄 Divide & Conquer

Recursively sorts the sub-arrays on both sides of the pivot until the entire array is sorted.

⏱️ Time Complexity

Best/Average: O(n log n). Worst: O(n²) when pivot is always min/max. Very efficient in practice.

⚡ In-Place Sorting

Uses minimal extra space. One of the fastest sorting algorithms for average cases.
